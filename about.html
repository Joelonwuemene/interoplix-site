About Interoplix

Helping healthcare organizations and technology partners get different systems working together—through practical integration expertise and dependable delivery support.

What we do

Healthcare runs on many different technologies. Interoplix helps the teams behind them connect applications, laboratory equipment and information flows. We focus on interoperability, interface troubleshooting, testing and implementation support, with AI integration and workflow automation as supporting capabilities.

Who we serve

We work with healthcare technology vendors, established IT consulting firms and healthcare organizations. We can contribute specialist integration expertise alongside an existing implementation team or support a defined technical project.

Leadership and experience

Interoplix is a founder-led company built on more than seven years of professional healthcare integration experience, including involvement in over 30 integration projects across 17 countries. The founder's background includes global diagnostics connectivity, HL7 and ASTM interfaces, LIS/LIMS workflows, technical troubleshooting and implementation coordination.

These experience figures refer to the founder's prior professional work, not contracts delivered by Interoplix LLC.

The founder holds an MSc in Medical Informatics and Microsoft Azure Administrator Associate and Azure Solutions Architect Expert certifications. View the founder's LinkedIn profile →
